⚠️ READ THIS BEFORE ENABLING.
Turning this on sends your data — including your resume's contents: your name, contact details, work history, and everything else in it — to an internet AI provider (OpenAI or Anthropic) using your API key.
  • Your data leaves this server. This feature does NOT keep your information private and local.
  • You are solely responsible for any personal information sent to the provider, for the provider's handling of it, and for any costs your API key incurs. We accept no liability.
  • Do not enable this unless you know exactly what's in your resume and are comfortable sharing it with the internet-based model.
