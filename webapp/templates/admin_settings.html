Job search

The default search depth pre-selected on every user's search form (users can still change it per search). Retries and scheduled alert searches also use this default. Deeper searches take longer and put more load on the job boards — and the boards themselves cap how far back they'll go (Indeed around two weeks); the internal job library is what accumulates older postings.
